# Shared topics across Märkisch-Oderland

**Checked 27 September 2026; observation window since 27 March 2025.** This is a source-linked regional discovery sample, **not** a claim that every county council system was indexed. The reproducible output is [`stadtstack-data/out/regions/brandenburg-mol/topics.json`](../../stadtstack-data/out/regions/brandenburg-mol/topics.json), produced with `npm --prefix stadtstack-data/regional run all`. The [adapter contract and rights boundaries](../../stadtstack-data/regional/README.md) explain the fields. All eight published topics are **editorial candidates**; a participation deadline is not a council resolution, an adopted *plan* is not a built project, and councils within a topic may be working on different projects.

## Result and concrete comparisons

The [Brandenburg municipal directory](https://service.brandenburg.de/service/de/adressen/kommunalverzeichnis/ansicht/~12064-maerkisch-oderland) lists **45** municipalities in Märkisch-Oderland (MOL), **33** in **six Ämter** and **12** amtsfreie. The published sample has **eight** cross-municipality topic categories, **19** distinct municipalities represented across those topics, and **48** item links (31 planning-procedure, 14 city-site, three council-agenda links). Source collection actually attributed **65** DiPlan procedures to **18** municipalities, **16** verified curated official-page assertions, and four Hoppegarten council items; 22 distinct municipalities had at least one collected item, including items without a second-city shared topic. The table below names the number of **municipalities**, not the number of plans, and links directly to examples rather than treating each topic as one joint initiative.

The name “Falkenberg” is **not** a safe municipality match on its own: [Falkenberg/Elster](https://www.falkenberg-elster.de/) belongs to Elbe-Elster, whereas the county here includes Falkenberg/Mark. The adapter explicitly excludes procedures titled Falkenberg/Elster; a DiPlan map pin/place can describe a neighbouring town or Ortsteil rather than the responsible municipality. A row with two official municipality names, such as the Podelzig–Lebus wind plans, is attributed only when the procedure specifies its particular “hier Stadt/Gemeinde” jurisdiction. The account titled “Amt Seelow-Land Gemeinde Falkenhagen” also handles many other Seelow-Land municipalities; it does **not** assign all its plans to Falkenhagen.

## Reproducibility and safeguards
 
The one-command script installs pinned dependencies, fetches public sources with a declared user agent, checks robots rules and crawl delays, caches responses locally (ignored by Git), verifies registry/curated phrases, and generates all 45 municipalities with county lookup. A date override `REGIONAL_AS_OF=YYYY-MM-DD` reproduces the 18-month filter; `npm --prefix stadtstack-data/regional run build -- --offline` regenerates using an already populated cache without requesting live upstreams. Only a topic appearing in **at least two distinct municipalities** is published. Ordered German title-keyword rules provide subject candidates; TF-IDF title clustering is restricted to otherwise uncategorized items and is still labelled a candidate. Generic “Bebauungsplan”/“Flächennutzungsplan” alone is **not** a meaningful shared policy topic.
 
**Rights and privacy:** Administrative contact addresses appearing in official source records are not resident addresses; none are published. The municipal-directory data is attributed under dl-de/by-2-0. DiPlan, RIS and city-site full-text/PDF/imagery licences were **not** established: publish an attributed short factual index with direct source URLs and locators rather than copying documents, describing source data as openly licensed, or presenting inferred statuses as adopted. The dataset has no personalisation or resident-interest field. Geometry inspection demonstrated a possible upstream plan-polygon route on two sampled pages only; no polygon is included, and approximate municipality address markers must never be drawn as plan boundaries.

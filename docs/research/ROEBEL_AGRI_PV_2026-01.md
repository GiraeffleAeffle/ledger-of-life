# Röbel/Müritz citizen Agri-PV proposal · January 2026

## Source and status

Source: **“Vorstellung Bürgerbeteiligung Agri-Photovoltaik in Röbel/Müritz”**, SolRenta Betriebs GmbH & Co. KG (Nuthetal), presented to the Röbel/Müritz building committee; slides dated **21 January 2026**. The presentation reached the project owner privately; no public online record was located. The presentation file is not published here.

**Developer’s figures, not verified; a proposal, not a decision.** This note attributes what the presentation describes; it does not establish approval, construction, availability of an investment product, or realized results. It is not legal advice or an offer.

## What was proposed

- **Variant 1:** 6 MWp Agri-PV, about 6 million kWh/year, described as equivalent to about 1,500 single-family homes; approximately €4 million investment.
- **Variant 2:** 8 MWp Agri-PV; approximately €6 million investment.
- Citizen participation through a bank’s crowd-investing product, **DKB-Crowd, from €250**. The town would decide eligibility: Röbel, the Amt, the district, the state, or nationwide.
- Cheaper electricity tariffs within **25 km**.
- Low-cost electricity for a heat concept (heat pump or district heating), a direct connection without grid fees, and approximately **5,000 tonnes of CO₂ avoided per year**, all as developer claims rather than verified effects.

### Process still required

The described next steps were approval by the building committee and town council, a development-plan (**B-Plan**) procedure, public participation, and then construction. A presentation is not a decision or permission to build.

## What tokenization could add — conditionally

A suitably regulated real project could use smaller participation tickets, transparent on-chain receipts, automatic payouts and reinvestment, and local eligibility checked through residence proof. These are possible accounting and access features, not a way around today’s crowd-investing regulation. Any real offer would still need the same regulatory assessment, defined legal rights, issuer evidence, risk disclosures and suitable eligibility arrangements.

Ledger of Life’s tHOME example demonstrates a separate **testnet-only** loop: attributed GPU test-dollar income and **simulated** solar feed-in income enter a building distributor; only staked fictional tHOME units accrue a seven-day reward stream. A holder can review a claim to their own wallet, buy fractional tHOME with the confirmed claimed tUSDG at the fixed test desk price, approve the exact units and stake them. Every transaction needs an own-wallet review. This is not a participation offer in the Röbel project. **Fictional units have no value, property title, company rights or claims on real infrastructure income.**

## Illustrative income model

Money → Local stakes has a separate **Fictional Agri-PV example · illustrative**, not an issuer, token or purchase. Editable defaults use SolRenta’s privately shared **21 January 2026** proposal above: variant 1 **6 MWp, about 6,000,000 kWh/year and €4,000,000 investment**; **8 ha** from its lease calculation; **€250** from the proposed participation minimum. Developer’s figures, not verified; a proposal, not a decision. The public attribution link is [this research note](https://github.com/GiraeffleAeffle/ledger-of-life/blob/develop/docs/research/ROEBEL_AGRI_PV_2026-01.md), not a publicly available presentation.

The editable electricity-price assumption is **€0.0479/kWh**, using the **4.79 ct/kWh volume-weighted award value** in the [Bundesnetzagentur first-segment solar tender of 1 July 2026](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/Ausschreibungen/Solaranlagen1/01072026/artikel.html), **published 18 August 2026**, reviewed 2 October 2026. This is an EEG support benchmark, not a sales-price quote, an Agri-PV premium, or an award to Röbel. The model assumes all production is sold and all 8 ha produce crops; plant size is context and does not automatically rescale the independently editable production input.

Crop revenue **€2,000/ha/year** is an invented editable example (**5 tonnes/ha × €400/tonne**, neither a measured yield nor a sourced producer price). Operating costs are an invented editable **20% of combined electricity and crop revenues**. The assumed common income pool is not the DKB-Crowd product’s legal payout model. Annual electricity revenue = sold kWh × assumed price; crop revenue = hectares × assumed revenue/ha; costs = combined revenues × cost share; each ticket gets ticket/investment of each amount. Costs are deducted once. Financing, tax, depreciation, reserves and legal distribution terms are excluded.

Hand check: **€287,400 electricity + €16,000 crops − €60,680 costs = €242,720/year**. A **€250/€4,000,000** share gives **€17.9625 electricity + €1 crops − €3.7925 costs = €15.17/year**, with a simple net-income/ticket ratio of **6.068%** (not IRR or compound return). These are **illustrative calculations with editable assumptions, not a forecast, offer, yield promise or investment advice**; no real holder entitlement is established.

## Where it feels assembled rather than composed

1. **Three city modules, three definitions of “latest”:** atlas `/api/city`, pipeline `/api/city-signals`, and `/api/places` live/static extras. The same atlas project appears in the city summary and signals; the same OSM place can appear in the pipeline and Overpass section; budgets appear as records and prose. Overview stacks two Places previews rather than one personal briefing.
2. **Mixed information scales:** “Near you” means home proximity in the map only with a pin; without it Overview shows citywide budget/project records, while “Clubs and sports near you” means *anywhere within the administrative boundary*. No verified city events or local news feed exists in today's app. Directories/world links and budget law can remain background detail, but compete with time-sensitive signals when placed as full-height cards.
3. **Repeated finance, disconnected journey:** Home claim balance, Money deposit tile, Overview dollar amount and Money's dedicated Invest card restate amounts. Devnet interest must be simulated via Test tools; tSPYx buy has a separate fixed 5-test-USDC action. Robinhood earn sits in Home Test tools while TSLA purchase sits in Money; neither collateral calculator nor `CollateralEscrow` is a real signed-in pledge action. This is a product handoff, not an absence of prototypes.
4. **Different confidence words:** Atlas `pending_review`/“not yet reviewed by the city” vs signal `candidate`/`auto_checked`; the compact card exposes raw `geometryPrecision=none` and numeric faithfulness instead of explaining “citywide; no precise location” and “automated source check, not approved.” `CityCard` calls its dated research item “latest”; an old paper or plan is not necessarily a new decision. A self-chosen city should not imply verified residence. `READ-ONLY LIVE` weather is a timed reading, not a city condition everywhere.
5. **Interface weight and dead ends:** On 390 px, Places stretches from controls/map and dense lists through five full explainer cards and repeated roadmap box; finding a relevant action is hard. Ideas gives a full long card to already built map, and mobile lacks an Ideas tab. “Show my city” updates atlas/places while the map's “Explore covered city” is temporary browsing; relationship needs clearer wording. The wide-scale links and Strausberg calendar/document cards are honest outbound references, not actual feeds. The morning screen still asks users to infer change from snapshots rather than compute *since their last visit*.

## Data-source boundary to preserve

`stadtstack-data/out/` is a public whole-city publication with stable `id`, normalized-content `version`, source locators, `reviewState`, geometry precision and previous-publish changes. `src/server/city-signals.ts` serves it through public-city-only API parameters; resident matching and optional pins/interests stay on the device. Atlas is independent human project interpretation, and feeds pipeline project records as inputs; CCF supplies upstream council source records, not decisions. No city is contacted by this proposal. Dashboard changes must carry source, as-of, review state, plan-versus-actual, simulated/test labels, and coverage gaps through to the visible card.
